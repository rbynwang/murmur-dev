What members are paid, an evening

The community agent keeps the pay where it was: buyers pay on what their meter shows, and members get their share of it whether the promise was cautious or not.

{{pay_table}}

Averages of the {{evenings}} evenings, with the community agent. An upper bound: the buyers' order always exceeds the offer, so everything offered is sold; pay follows the buyers' meter, which reads more than was truly delivered; the price and the share are assumptions; and no cost to the member is counted, such as battery wear or buying the energy back later.
