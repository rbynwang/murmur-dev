Murmur · the architecture, its data, and one recorded evening

AI agents in every home, put to work on the grid's evening peak

The premise

AI agents will enter every household and every part of our lives, and make them run more efficiently.

The goal, for a utility company

Use those agents to shave the grid's peak: less draw from homes, buildings and other sites, from 18:00 to 21:00.

5

What is real

Said once, in one place.

Real

ENGIE's 2025 records, and our code. Each agent is a separate program. Their messages are signed and cross a NANDA Town run on one computer. A real small model reads the notes people write.

Simulated

Homes, buildings and their devices, each from a real product's ratings. A command travels the road it would travel to real equipment. Only the last step, the driver, is simulated.

Replayed

The members' batteries and the single sites' own power, from ENGIE's record.

Stand-ins and assumptions

The buyers and the aggregator's own agent are stand-ins. The price of a cut, $2.04 per kWh, is ours, with a range of $1.53 to $3.06.

Murmur, 20 November 2026. Figures come from ENGIE's 2025 dataset, the product catalogue, and the evening recorded on 20 November 2026.
