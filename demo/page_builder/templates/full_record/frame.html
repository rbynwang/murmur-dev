◆ Who decides what

In the record of 11 Nov 2026: one of ENGIE's evenings in January 2025, with eight homes, a small office and shops of its community 19 and a stand-in for the aggregator's own agent on a local NANDA Town. This code isn't on the 200-home live system yet.

  • The 8 nodes. Each home has its own agent, and code makes every one of its plans: the offer, the commitment, the sale's opening and each exception. Each receipt reads "rule: plans are decided by code".
  • The offer. Code's planner works out the safe amount inside the person's limits, and offers it. A need read from a note lowers it, and the person is told.
  • Sizing. Code picks the devices and works out each one's exact kW.
  • The guard. Code. It checks every device call against the safety and money limits and what a person asked to keep, and it has the last word.
  • The small model. A stand-in answered in its place in this evening: it worded each home's report and made no plan. On the eight scenario days the reader was Qwen 3.5 4B.
  • The aggregator. Shares one grid need among 80 participants and asks each community for its part. Here a stand-in for its own agent sends its request for the members' batteries as a signed message, and receives the community's signed answer and its report. It sends a list it is given: it does not share out the need itself. Two stand-in desks with fixed orders still carry the order to the homes and buildings.
  • The utility. Sets a limit at the community's connection, which can overrule a sale. The limit is set in this evening and checked at the clearing.
  • Community agent. By code, with its community switch on. It adds the offers up, promises only what its own record of finished evenings says its members deliver, clears the sale, keeps to the utility's limit, answers the aggregator's signed request for the members' batteries (it takes it, or refuses it and says why), sends its report upward one signed row a quarter hour, checks the readings and pays, keeping a fixed 20%. No AI decides anything here. The record below calls it the coordinator.
  • The same evening before. The same evening ran this morning with the request given on the same computer and the report not sent. Every amount here equals that run's.
